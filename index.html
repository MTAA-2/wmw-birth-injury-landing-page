Birth Injuries

Birth Injuries May Require a Lifetime of Medical Care

Cerebral Palsy is a type of brain injury, often caused by decreased oxygen and/or blood flow to the baby, resulting in a neurologic disorder that can lead to significant physical and mental disabilities for the child.

Erb's Palsy is a condition that results from injury to the nerves that control the muscles in the shoulder, arms or hand. The goal of most doctors is to get the child's arm moving normally by age two.

Both of these conditions can require expensive care and treatment to maximize the child's quality of life and their chances for improvement. These can include:

  • Long-term residential care and in-home supervision
  • Therapies and assistive devices
  • Medications and medical specialists
  • Home modifications and surgeries

To provide the best future possible for their children and to relieve this financial burden, families will often pursue compensation from the responsible parties.

How It Happens

You Might Be Entitled to Compensation

While there are some instances in which a birth injury occurs for no obvious reason and no one is to blame, it may at times be caused by the following, among other things:

Delayed Emergency C-Section

A physician failing to plan and carry out an emergency C-section.

Excessive Force in Delivery

The child's arm being pulled too forcefully during delivery.

Missed Signs of Distress

Lack of careful monitoring for signs of distress.

Improper Use of Delivery Tools

Improper use of tools used for assisting with the child's birth.

Untreated Maternal Infection

Failure to diagnose or properly treat maternal infection.

Post-Birth Monitoring Failures

Failure to properly monitor and/or treat the baby's respiratory status post birth.
